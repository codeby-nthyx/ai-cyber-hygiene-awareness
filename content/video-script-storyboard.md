# Awareness Video: Pause, Check, Protect

**Target duration:** 45–55 seconds  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 social video  
**Audience:** Students aged 15–24  
**Style:** Fast, clean motion graphics; readable captions; no real personal data

| Time | Visual / on-screen text | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 sec | Student using phone and laptop. Text: “Your digital life needs daily care.” | Your assignments, accounts and memories live on your devices. Give them a little protection. |
| 5–13 sec | Update icon; settings screen illustration. Text: “1. UPDATE SAFELY” | Install updates through trusted settings or official stores—not random pop-ups or links. |
| 13–21 sec | Two copies of a coursework folder, one separate. Text: “2. BACK UP + TEST” | Keep a second copy of important files, and check that you can restore it. |
| 21–29 sec | App permission toggles. Text: “3. LIMIT PERMISSIONS” | Give apps only the access their features need. Review permissions regularly. |
| 29–37 sec | Public Wi-Fi symbol and network-name check. Text: “4. CHECK THE NETWORK” | Confirm public Wi-Fi details with the venue. A VPN does not make every site trustworthy. |
| 37–46 sec | Browser address bar; domain highlighted. Text: “5. CHECK THE LINK” | Inspect the domain before signing in. HTTPS encrypts a connection; it does not prove the site is genuine. |
| 46–53 sec | Five icons appear together. Text: “PAUSE • CHECK • PROTECT” | Five small habits can make your digital routine safer. Start with one today. |

## Production notes
- Use fictional interface mock-ups; do not show real accounts, messages, phone numbers or credentials.
- Add accurate subtitles and ensure each key instruction remains on screen long enough to read.
- Use royalty-free or original music only, or publish without music.
- Include a final source/credits frame linking to the campaign repository and primary guidance.
