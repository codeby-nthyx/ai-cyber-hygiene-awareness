# Video-generation prompt

**Recommended tool:** Canva Video Editor (or CapCut if you prefer editing on desktop/mobile). Use the existing storyboard as the timing and narration reference. Generate or select visuals, then assemble and export a vertical 9:16 MP4. Keep the captions editable so factual wording can be checked before export.

## Master prompt

Create a 45–55 second vertical 9:16 educational social video titled **“Pause, Check, Protect”** for students aged 15–24, including non-technical learners. The tone should be clear, modern, energetic and trustworthy—not frightening or sensational. Use clean motion graphics, simple flat icons, smooth transitions, high-contrast readable captions and a consistent navy, cyan and white palette. Use fictional student/device scenes and generic interface mock-ups only. Do not show real accounts, personal data, credentials, brand logos, real websites or fake security alerts.

Structure the video into these six timed scenes:

1. **0–5 seconds — Opening:** A student using a phone and laptop. On-screen text: “Your digital life needs daily care.” Voice-over: “Your assignments, accounts and memories live on your devices. Give them a little protection.”
2. **5–13 seconds — Updates:** Show a software update icon and generic settings screen. On-screen text: “1. UPDATE SAFELY”. Voice-over: “Install updates through trusted settings or official stores—not random pop-ups or links.”
3. **13–21 seconds — Backups:** Show a coursework folder with a separate second copy and a restore/check symbol. On-screen text: “2. BACK UP + TEST”. Voice-over: “Keep a second copy of important files, and check that you can restore it.”
4. **21–29 seconds — Permissions:** Show simple camera, microphone and location permission toggles. On-screen text: “3. LIMIT PERMISSIONS”. Voice-over: “Give apps only the access their features need. Review permissions regularly.”
5. **29–37 seconds — Public Wi-Fi:** Show a public Wi-Fi symbol and a student confirming the network name with venue staff. On-screen text: “4. CHECK THE NETWORK”. Voice-over: “Confirm public Wi-Fi details with the venue. A VPN does not make every site trustworthy.”
6. **37–46 seconds — Links:** Show a generic browser address bar with the domain highlighted; do not display a real domain. On-screen text: “5. CHECK THE LINK”. Voice-over: “Inspect the domain before signing in. HTTPS encrypts a connection; it does not prove the site is genuine.”
7. **46–53 seconds — Closing:** Bring together five simple icons. On-screen text: “PAUSE • CHECK • PROTECT”. Voice-over: “Five small habits can make your digital routine safer. Start with one today.”

Add accurate subtitles matching the narration. Keep each key instruction on screen long enough to read. Use original or royalty-free music only, at a low volume under the narration, or no music. End with a small source/credits frame directing viewers to the campaign repository. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, endorsements or claims of guaranteed protection. Export as MP4, 1080 × 1920 if available.

## Final checks before export

- Confirm every caption matches the approved script and contains no spelling errors.
- Check that the HTTPS caveat and VPN limitation remain accurate.
- Use only fictional interface screens and non-identifying visuals.
- Confirm music and visual assets are licensed for the intended use.
- Watch the complete export once with sound and once muted to check subtitles.
