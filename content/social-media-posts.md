# Six Social Media Posts

All posts are draft educational copy. Add campaign branding and verify the final platform-specific layout before publication.

## Post 1 — Update smart
**Headline:** Your update reminder is a security reminder.  
**Copy:** Software updates can include important security fixes. Update your phone, laptop, browser and apps through built-in settings or official stores. Ignore unexpected update links in pop-ups and messages.  
**CTA:** Check your update settings today.  
**Hashtags:** #CyberHygiene #StayUpdated #StudentSafety

## Post 2 — Back up your work
**Headline:** One copy is one point of failure.  
**Copy:** Keep important assignments and personal files in a second, recoverable location. Understand the difference between sync and backup, and test that you can restore a file.  
**CTA:** Back up one important folder today.  
**Hashtags:** #BackupYourData #CyberSmart #StudentLife

## Post 3 — Permission check
**Headline:** Does that app really need access?  
**Copy:** Review camera, microphone, location, contacts and photo permissions. Grant only what the feature needs, and remove access that no longer makes sense.  
**CTA:** Open your app-permission settings.  
**Hashtags:** #PrivacyMatters #AppPermissions #CyberHygiene

## Post 4 — Public Wi-Fi
**Headline:** Free Wi-Fi does not mean risk-free Wi-Fi.  
**Copy:** Confirm the network name with the venue. Keep your device updated and use secure websites. A VPN can help protect network traffic, but it cannot make a phishing page legitimate.  
**CTA:** Think before connecting.  
**Hashtags:** #PublicWiFi #OnlineSafety #CyberAware

## Post 5 — Link check
**Headline:** Pause before you tap.  
**Copy:** Unexpected urgency, prizes and sign-in requests are warning signs. Inspect the actual domain. For important services, use the official app or type the known address yourself. HTTPS alone does not prove a site is genuine.  
**CTA:** Check the destination, not just the padlock.  
**Hashtags:** #PhishingAwareness #SafeBrowsing #ThinkBeforeYouClick

## Post 6 — Five habits challenge
**Headline:** Five habits. One safer digital routine.  
**Copy:** Update through trusted channels. Keep recoverable backups. Limit app permissions. Treat public Wi-Fi carefully. Check links and domains. Small actions, repeated consistently, improve your cyber hygiene.  
**CTA:** Pick one habit and practise it today.  
**Hashtags:** #CyberHygiene #DigitalWellbeing #StudentAwareness
