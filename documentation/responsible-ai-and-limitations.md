# Responsible AI, Privacy and Safety

## Permitted use
AI is used to assist with public-source research planning, summarisation, drafting, rewriting, quiz creation, consistency checks and formatting. Human review is required before publication.

## Privacy rules
- Do not upload student names, IDs, attendance records, contact details or identifiable photographs.
- Do not submit passwords, authentication codes, private messages or account recovery details.
- Do not upload confidential college documents, internal network diagrams, source code or security logs.
- Use fictional examples and public information.
- Obtain appropriate consent before collecting any audience feedback or quiz data; collect only what is necessary.

## Safety boundaries
This project is defensive awareness education. It does not create malware, exploit code, credential-harvesting pages, phishing kits or instructions for unauthorised access. Demonstrations use fictional mock-ups and benign examples only.

## Human oversight
The reviewer is responsible for:
- Checking every factual claim against its source.
- Removing fabricated citations, statistics and quotations.
- Preserving limitations and caveats across formats.
- Checking readability, accessibility and cultural appropriateness.
- Approving the final content before publication.

## Transparency
AI assistance should be disclosed in the project report. Tool names, versions, dates and exact uses must reflect what was actually used. Do not claim to have used a tool or performed a review without evidence.

## Known limitations
AI can produce plausible but false claims, outdated instructions, invented references, overgeneralised advice and content that omits important caveats. Official guidance can also change. The campaign is general education and is not a guarantee of safety or a substitute for institutional policies or professional incident response.
