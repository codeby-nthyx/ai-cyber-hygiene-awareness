# Prompt Library

Use these prompts with fictional examples and public information only. Do not submit passwords, personal records, private messages, student data or confidential institutional information.

## P1 — Research planner
**Prompt:**  
Act as a research assistant for a student cyber-hygiene awareness campaign for ages 15–24. Create a research checklist for software updates, backups, app permissions, public Wi-Fi and safe browsing. For each topic, list the specific claim that needs verification and the type of authoritative source to consult. Do not invent citations, statistics or quotations. Mark uncertain points as [VERIFY].

**Expected use:** Build a research plan, not a final factual answer.

## P2 — Plain-language explainer
**Prompt:**  
Explain [TOPIC] to a student aged 15–24 who has no cybersecurity background. Use a short everyday example, three practical actions and one limitation or caveat. Avoid fear-based language, absolute guarantees and unexplained jargon. Do not invent product-specific menu paths.

## P3 — Evidence checker
**Prompt:**  
Review the following draft claims against only the source excerpts I provide. For every claim, classify it as supported, partly supported, unsupported or contradicted. Quote the relevant short source wording, explain any mismatch, and propose a narrower replacement. If the excerpt does not contain enough evidence, say so. Do not use outside knowledge to fill gaps.

## P4 — Social post adaptation
**Prompt:**  
Convert this source-checked message into one social-media post for students aged 15–24. Preserve all caveats and the original meaning. Include a concise hook, one action and a non-alarmist call to action. Maximum 70 words. Do not add statistics or new security claims.

## P5 — Quiz generation
**Prompt:**  
Create one multiple-choice question from the verified learning point below. Provide four plausible options, one unambiguous correct answer and a short explanation. Do not test trivia or add facts not present in the learning point.

## P6 — Hallucination and safety audit
**Prompt:**  
Audit this campaign draft for invented statistics, fake citations, absolute claims, incorrect technical implications, unsafe instructions, privacy risks and misleading simplifications. Return a table with the exact text, issue, severity, evidence needed and a corrected version. Do not claim a source was checked unless its supplied text is present.

## P7 — Consistency check
**Prompt:**  
Compare these campaign assets. Identify contradictions in the five core behaviours, terminology, caveats and calls to action. Do not rewrite correct technical content. List only specific inconsistencies and proposed corrections.

## P8 — Reflection
**Prompt:**  
Based only on the actual workflow notes and evidence I provide, draft a reflection on where AI saved time, where it introduced errors or ambiguity, what human verification changed, and what remains untested. Do not invent user feedback, measured impact or tool usage.
